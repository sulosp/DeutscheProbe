# Ambience

The listening clips do not use recorded ambience.

Every bed is generated in this repository while the audio is built (`scripts/tts/build-audio.py`, ffmpeg `anoisesrc`). The exam signal is a generated sine tone in the same script. Nothing here is taken from a sample library, and there is no third-party recording to license.

Speech stays in front. The bed is mixed quietly, then a clip that contains speech is normalised to about −19 LUFS. The exam signal is not normalised on its own. Reading pauses in the player stay silent.
