# Ambience

The listening clips do not use recorded ambience.

Every bed, station chime and radio sting is generated in this repository while the audio is built (`scripts/tts/build-audio.py`, ffmpeg `anoisesrc` and `sine`). Nothing here is taken from a sample library, and there is no third-party recording to license.

Speech stays in front. The bed is mixed quietly, then the whole clip is normalised to about −16 LUFS.
